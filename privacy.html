Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Kiln is run by Nathan Stuckey. You can reach us at our contact address.

2. What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored in protected form, so we cannot see them.
  • Profile details. Your username and an optional short bio. These are shown publicly on your profile page.
  • Listings. Titles, descriptions, tags, links, prices, and images you upload. These are shown publicly.
  • Usage counts. How many times a game page was viewed and how many times each store button was clicked, stored as daily totals for each game. These counts are not linked to who you are.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and service providers may log information such as your IP address and browser type as part of running their services.

3. What is stored in your browser

Kiln stores a login session so you stay signed in, your color theme choice, and short-lived markers that stop the same visit from being counted twice. We do not set advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not currently run analytics or ads. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

4. How we use it

We use your information to run accounts and listings, show games to visitors, count views and clicks, send account emails such as confirmations and password resets, keep Kiln safe, and respond to your messages. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Who we share it with

We use Supabase to provide our database, sign-in, file storage, and account emails, and GitHub to host the website. They process information on our behalf. Anything you put in a public profile or listing can be seen by anyone.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account information while your account exists. You can edit your profile and delete your listings at any time. To delete your account and profile, email us and we will handle the request within a reasonable time. Copies in backups may remain for a short time.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and you can use the same address to ask about them.

8. Children

Kiln is not for children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

9. Other websites

Kiln links to other sites. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for what they do.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no website can be completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email our contact address.
